# Verifying IlonaTv on a TV

Manual checks for a build on real hardware, mostly a Fire TV, with notes for Google TV. They drive a live TV, so run them when nobody is watching (TV awake, nothing playing), from the repo root, after:

```sh
TV=<tv-ip>:5555
adb connect $TV
```

A command that does not start with `adb` runs inside `adb -s $TV shell`. Items marked **(changes the TV)** alter the TV's state (accessibility settings, reboots, force-stops); run them only on a TV you may change. `<other-app>` stands for any installed app's package, `<youtube-app>` for the app chosen under Settings > Open with > YouTube.

## Install, wizard and home (no keys)

1. **Install and first run.** `adb -s $TV install -r build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk` (or the `arm64-v8a` APK), then `adb -s $TV shell am start -n tv.ilona.app/.MainActivity`. Expected on a fresh install: the setup wizard, focus on its first row. Walk every step with the D-pad only: Left reaches Continue, OK advances, Back goes one step back, Skip on every step but the first and the last. Done shows the home with the dock built from the apps chosen.
2. **Home basics (Launcher mode off).** `input keyevent 22` three times, `screencap -p /sdcard/focus.png`, `adb -s $TV pull /sdcard/focus.png /tmp`, `rm /sdcard/focus.png`. Expected: the fourth tile focused, lifted, scaled up and with a thin bright edge, no green window frame; note how long the row takes to appear after the clock (a cold `listApps`). Then `input keyevent 4; sleep 1; dumpsys activity activities | grep mResumedActivity` gives `tv.ilona.app` still. OK on a tile brings its app to the front within 3 s.
3. **Settings on the TV.** Menu opens Settings; Right and Left between the list and the pane, Left from a row's first action returns to the entry; the Choose app and Choose icon dialogs close with Back; the language switches to Portuguese and back; About shows memory, the licence and the TMDB and JustWatch credits, and "Run setup again" opens the wizard, which Back leaves. TV settings on Fire OS lists Network, Applications, Controllers & Bluetooth, My Fire TV and Accessibility (each opens its own page, Back returns to IlonaTv) and All settings (the Amazon home's settings tab, which stays up until Home brings IlonaTv back).
4. **Memory, mode off.** `adb -s $TV shell dumpsys meminfo tv.ilona.app | grep TOTAL` with the home settled (under 95 MB), right after opening the Apps grid, and settled again; note Native Heap.

## Launcher mode

5. **(changes the TV)** Enable the service. `settings get secure enabled_accessibility_services` and note what it prints. In IlonaTv: Settings > Launcher mode > On. Expected: the status line reads "Accessibility service: off", and the pane shows `adb connect` and `adb -s … shell pm grant …` with the TV's address, and "Check again". Then `pm grant tv.ilona.app android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`, and "Check again". Expected: "Accessibility service: on", and `get` prints what it printed before plus `tv.ilona.app/.HomeWatcher` at the end. Switch Off and On again: IlonaTv's entry goes and comes back, and the others stay. Open IlonaTv once so it sits under "Recently used apps".
6. **Home brings IlonaTv back**, three times: `adb -s $TV shell 'logcat -c; monkey -p <other-app> 1; sleep 4; input keyevent 3; sleep 6; logcat -d -s IlonaTv'`, with `screenrecord --time-limit 8 /sdcard/home.mp4` running in parallel once. Expected: one `curtain up +≤100ms`, `click 'IlonaTv' ok`, `curtain down (drawn|resumed) +≤2500ms`; count the `home event` lines per press; no launcher frame between curtain-down and IlonaTv in the recording. `logcat -d -s flutter | grep 'snapshot failed'` must be empty.
7. **Edge cases.** Double Home (`input keyevent 3; sleep 0.5; input keyevent 3`) leaves IlonaTv in front; with the mode off there are no `curtain` or `click` lines and the Amazon home stays; with the tile missing (rebuild with `"IlonaTvx"` as the label in `HomeWatcher.onHome`, reinstall, restore) the log shows `tile 'IlonaTvx' not found` and `curtain down (drawn)` near +5000 ms, never `(timeout)`.
8. **Queued-start reset.** Home, wait for IlonaTv, press Menu within 3 s, wait until +8 s. Expected: Settings still open; logcat shows whether a second `onNewIntent` or goHome arrived.
9. **Keys during the curtain.** Home, then `input keyevent 23` at +0.3 s; note what the Amazon home does, and the median curtain duration from check 6.
10. **Launcher windows.** With the mode on, long-press Home: does the quick-access menu appear and work, or does the curtain cover it? Then `logcat -s IlonaTv` through 10 minutes of video in `<youtube-app>` and one sleep and wake: any `curtain up` not tied to a Home press?
11. **App launch during the lock.** IlonaTv in front with an app tile focused: `logcat -c; input keyevent 3; sleep 3; input keyevent 23; sleep 3; logcat -d -s IlonaTv; dumpsys activity activities | grep mResumedActivity`. Records whether Fire OS holds a foreground app's start within 5 s of Home.
12. **(changes the TV)** Boot. `adb -s $TV reboot`; after boot, `adb connect $TV` and `dumpsys activity activities | grep mResumedActivity` gives `tv.ilona.app` without touching the remote; note how long the Amazon home was visible.
13. **Memory with the service on**, home settled and after the Apps grid: under 95 MB, 120 MB at most.
14. **Wake from sleep** with the mode on: Amazon home, curtain, IlonaTv; note the timing.
15. **Remote options.** "Remote sends power twice": with a remote that sends power over IR and Bluetooth, one press turns the TV off and it stays off. "Mic button opens IlonaTv": the mic button shows IlonaTv's assistant (Alexa may flash first); with a Gemini key, a spoken question gets a spoken answer, and Back closes the overlay.
16. **Google TV.** `adb install -r build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`, press Home. Step 1 of the README (the chooser, "Always"); if there is none, step 2 (Settings > Launcher mode > On opens the system's accessibility settings, turn IlonaTv on; if it is greyed out, "Open app info", allow restricted settings, then "Open accessibility settings"), Home again; if still not, **(changes the TV)** step 3, `pm disable-user --user 0` on `launcherx` and `setupwraith`, Home again, and note the YouTube button. Note which step worked; confirm Back on the home does nothing and that the Apps grid sees apps.

## Content (keys and accounts)

These need a TMDB key typed on the TV and accounts approved from a phone.

17. **Keys and accounts.** Settings > Accounts, type the TMDB key. Expected: Test says "Key works" (a wrong key says "Key rejected"), and the key shows masked after Save. Then YouTube > Add account, approve the code at google.com/device on a phone. Expected: the profiles of that login, ticked; Add lists them with their switches on.
18. **Rows.** After saving the TMDB key, go back to the home and time it: the backdrop and "Trending · Movies", "Trending · Series" and "Recommended" are up within 10 s. The YouTube row follows the YouTube profiles the same way.
19. **Backdrop.** Leave the app row focused for a minute: a new backdrop every ~10 s, crossfading with no stutter, and no change while a card has focus (the backdrop follows the card instead).
20. **Moving.** Down from the app row parks the first row under the Highlight; Right and Left over its cards; Down and Up between rows; Up back to the app row. Expected: the Highlight and the backdrop follow the focused card, each row remembers its card, the clock and weather stay, and Back from a row returns to the top.
21. **Opening in a Stremio app.** With a Stremio app under Settings > Open with: `adb -s $TV shell 'logcat -c'`, OK on a movie, then `adb -s $TV shell "logcat -d | grep 'START u0'"`. Expected: the app in front on that title's page, not on its home (the intent is `stremio:///detail/movie/tt...`; Android 14 prints only `stremio:///...` there, so the page on screen is the check). With the official Stremio Android TV app and "Play the first stream" on, the first stream starts. `logcat -d -s IlonaTv` shows `startActivity failed` when a link opened nothing.
22. **Opening in streaming apps.** Set Movies to "My streaming apps" and map at least one installed service under Streaming services. Expected: the movie row shows titles on that service; the Highlight's button reads "Watch on <service>"; OK launches that service's app (on its own home). A title on no mapped service shows "No app could open this".
23. **Videos.** OK on a YouTube video opens it in `<youtube-app>`, on that video; with no YouTube app chosen, in whatever app Android picks. Holding OK on a movie card counts down and plays its trailer.
24. **Stremio.** Settings > Accounts > Stremio > Log in with your phone. Expected: a QR and a four-character code; approve on the phone and within a few seconds the pane reads "Logged in as <email>" and "Continue watching" is the first row, with series showing "S2 · E5" under the name ("T2 · E5" in Portuguese); OK on one opens the Stremio app on that episode. Cancel, or leaving the pane, ends the wait; with no approval the code is replaced after 5 minutes and the pane gives up after 15.
25. **Keyboard.** Right or Down into a field opens nothing; OK opens the keyboard; Back closes it and the field stops accepting keys; the arrows leave the field. With the keyboard up, Left, Right, Up and Down move the keyboard's own focus, not the pane's. The keyboard's Next closes it and puts focus on Save. If a field still takes keys after Back, Fire OS reports its keyboard differently from what `_Field` in `lib/ui/settings_panes.dart` expects (it watches the window's bottom inset).
26. **Curtain with the rows up** (needs check 5): focus a card in a row, run `screenrecord --time-limit 8 /sdcard/home.mp4` in parallel, press `input keyevent 3` once, then pull and remove the file. Expected: the curtain frames show the backdrop and the row, not the plain gradient, and no half-faded frame. Try again with Home pressed just as the backdrop changes, and with Down then Home within about 200 ms (`input keyevent 20 3`).
27. **Memory with the rows loaded.** With the Apps grid opened once and the accessibility service on, make one pass Down through every row and back, then leave the app row focused for over 3 minutes (two backdrop cycles), and take three readings 20 s apart with `adb -s $TV shell dumpsys meminfo tv.ilona.app`. Expected: Private Dirty at or under 85 MB, GL + EGL mtrack at or under 50 MB, TOTAL under 120 MB with no climb from the first reading to the third, and no stutter or `am_kill` of the playing app when going to another app and back. TOTAL PSS also counts file pages the kernel reclaims, so it is only a reference.
28. **A large library.** With a Stremio account that has hundreds of library items, leave the home up for over 15 minutes: no hitch in the crossfade or the D-pad when the 15-minute refresh runs, and no jump in memory afterwards.
29. **Weather.** With a city set in Settings > Weather, the line "<city> · <icon> N° · feels like N°" appears under the clock within 3 s of a cold start; switching the unit to °F refetches it in °F. With no city, no weather line and no separator. **(changes the TV)** `am force-stop tv.ilona.app` then `am start -n tv.ilona.app/.MainActivity`: the line is there on the first frame, and Settings > Launcher mode reads "Accessibility service: on" again.
30. **Refresh under focus.** Focus a "Continue watching" card and leave it there; on a phone signed in to the same Stremio account, play a few minutes of another title; wait past the next 15-minute refresh. Expected: the Highlight names the card under the ring, and OK opens that same title.
31. **A library item not on IMDb** (an anime from the Kitsu add-on, id `kitsu:...`): OK on its "Continue watching" card opens the Stremio app on that title, not on its home.
32. **A rejected TMDB key.** Save a wrong TMDB key (not just Test), then go back to the home: a hint under the weather, "Your TMDB key was rejected. Check it in Settings." Save the right key again afterwards.
33. **Long press on a card** with no trailer: hold OK for 4 s, then release. Expected: nothing opens. A short press on the same card opens it.
34. **Large text (Google TV).** In the system's Accessibility settings, set text scaling to its largest step, then Down into a row on the home. Expected: the row's title does not overlap the Highlight's button. Set the text size back afterwards.
35. **(changes the TV)** Network down at boot. With the network down, reboot the TV. Expected: the rows show from the caches. Bring the network back, open another app and press Home to come back: the rows refresh as each source's cache falls due (15 minutes for Stremio, 30 for the weather and YouTube, 6 hours for TMDB).
36. **Holding Right** across "Trending · Movies", then letting go: the backdrop and the Highlight catch up with the card under the ring within about 1 s.

## Sports

37. **Following.** Settings > Sports: OK on a league, Follow on; the row value reads "All games". Tick two teams and turn on Only my teams: the value reads "2 teams", and the home's Games row shows only their games, today's and tomorrow's. Following an 8th league works; a 9th is refused with a note.
38. **Opening a game.** With a Stremio account whose add-ons include a sports catalog and a Stremio app chosen for movies or series, OK on a game opens its item in that app. Without one, the home says no app could open it.

## Companion server

The TV answers on port 8642. From a laptop on the same network, with `TVIP=<tv-ip>` and `A="http://$TVIP:8642"`. The pairing code and token below are placeholders.

39. **Alive.** `curl $A/` prints a small HTML page saying `IlonaTv is running`.
40. **Closed without a token.** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}\n' $A/api/info` prints `401`.
41. **Pairing.** Open Settings > Companion on the TV (or, on a first run, the wizard's first three steps) and leave it on screen: pairing by code only works while one of them is showing. Read the code off the screen, then `curl -X POST $A/api/pair -d '{"code":"<code>","name":"laptop"}'`. Expected: `{"token":"<token>"}`. A wrong code, or the right one with the TV on another screen, gives `403`. Then `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" $A/api/info`: the protocol, the version, `step` (the wizard step on screen, or null), `inFront` and `launcherMode`. Pairing again with the QR's secret gives `403`: each QR pairs once, and the TV shows a new one.
42. **Keys.** `curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" -d '{"key":"right"}' $A/api/key` answers `204` and focus moves right on the TV. Repeat for `left`, `up`, `down`, `ok` and `back`. An unknown key answers `400`.
43. **Open, Launcher mode on.** With Launcher mode on, open Netflix (or any other app) so IlonaTv is behind it, then pipe the first item of the first row into `/api/open`: `curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" $A/api/rows | jq -c '.[0].items[0]' | jq -c '{item: .}' | curl -s -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" -d @- $A/api/open`. Expected: `{"opened":true}` and the title's app in front within 3 s. `/api/rows` is `[{"key","title","items":[...]}]`; `/api/open` takes `{"item": <one of those items>}`.
44. **Open, Launcher mode off.** Same with Launcher mode off. With another app in front, `/api/open` and `/api/key` both answer `409` (`notInFront`): only the home app may start activities from the background.
45. **Still answering after 30 minutes.** Leave the TV in Netflix for 30 minutes, then `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" $A/api/info`. Record whether the server still answers. This is the spec's open risk: if it does not, part 2's plan starts with keeping the engine alive.
46. **Without the activity.** Developer options > "Don't keep activities" on. Open another app, then `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" $A/api/info`. Record whether the server outlives IlonaTv's activity (the spec's open risk), and whether `dns-sd -B _ilonatv._tcp` (or `avahi-browse`) still lists IlonaTv while the server is gone. Turn the option off after.
47. **Memory.** `adb -s $TV shell dumpsys meminfo tv.ilona.app | grep TOTAL` before and after the server is up: under 5 MB difference.
48. **Discovery.** On the laptop's network, `dns-sd -B _ilonatv._tcp` (macOS) or `avahi-browse -r _ilonatv._tcp` (Linux) lists "IlonaTv on <model>", port 8642.

## Companion app

Run these with the companion APK on an Android phone on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. Item 49 starts from a Fire TV without IlonaTv installed; the others use the IlonaTv it installed.

49. **Install from the phone. (changes the TV)** With ADB debugging on, open the companion, choose to install, and pick the TV from the scan (or type its IP). Expected: the TV shows "Allow debugging?" and the phone says to choose Allow; after Allow the Check step shows the latest release's version, the phone downloads the APK for the TV's CPU from it (with MB of MB), installs it, starts IlonaTv and offers "Open the remote". Then the same with the prompt denied: the phone says the TV didn't allow the connection, and Retry shows a new prompt on the TV. Run the installer again on the same TV: Check says it already has this version, and Next goes straight to pairing. With the phone's mobile data and Wi-Fi internet cut (the TV still on the LAN), Install says it can't reach GitHub, with Retry.
50. **Pair by QR and by code.** On the TV, Settings > Companion. Scan the QR from the companion: the TV appears in the phone's list. Remove it, then pair again by typing the code. A QR that was used once does not pair a second time. The installer's own pairing needs neither.
51. **Remote keys.** Up, Down, Left, Right, OK, Back and Home on the phone move focus on the TV. With another app in front and Launcher mode off, the phone says "Open IlonaTv on the TV" and offers the Launcher-mode tip.
52. **Search and Watch, IlonaTv in front.** Search for a title on the phone, open it and press Watch: the TV opens it in the app it uses for that title. Browse a row and do the same.
53. **Search and Watch, another app in front.** With Netflix (or any app) in front and Launcher mode on, Watch brings the title's app forward within 3 s. With Launcher mode off, the phone says "Open IlonaTv on the TV" and nothing opens.
54. **Typing during setup.** Start the wizard on a TV; on a step with a text field (TMDB key, city), the phone shows the matching card (the TMDB key, or a city search). The TV receives the key only on Test or Save, and the city only when you tap a result.
55. **Remove on the TV.** In Settings > Companion on the TV, Remove the phone. Expected: the next key press or search on the phone shows "This TV removed this phone. Pair again." and the TV is listed as unpaired; pairing again works. With the TV off or unplugged, the phone shows "Can't reach <name>" with Retry, not a spinner.
56. **ADB can go off.** After 49, turn ADB debugging off on the TV: the remote, search and Watch still work.
